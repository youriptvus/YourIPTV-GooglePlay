# Reviewer access instructions

YourIPTV / com.youriptv.app. Developer/support/privacy: YourIPTV, admin@youriptv.us, https://youriptv.us. One app serves phones, tablets and Android TV. No YourIPTV registration, purchase or channel subscription is required.

Public authorized demo: https://youriptv.us/review-media/playlist.m3u
Rolling XMLTV guide: https://youriptv.us/review-media/epg.php
Privacy: https://youriptv.us/privacy

These HTTPS endpoints were independently checked on November 18, 2026. The generated-media audit imports the public catalogue/guide and checks actual decoded frames for live/movie/episode playback on the isolated phone and TV. Exact results and limitations are in Reports/Accessibility-and-Public-Playback.md. Keep access working throughout review; this is not a worldwide uptime guarantee.

1. Open YourIPTV. Open Settings, then Playlists. Fresh Play Settings opens without a PIN.
2. Add an M3U playlist named YourIPTV Review using the public playlist URL above. Enter the rolling guide URL if it is not automatically advertised. Save and refresh.
3. Select a generated live item in the guide and open fullscreen. Use touch on a phone or D-pad/OK/Back on TV. After opening both items in one fullscreen queue, Right recalls the prior channel and another Right toggles back.
4. Use Update Playlist and Update EPG in Settings and return to the guide.
5. Open the generated movie and series episode; each is a short synthetic clip. They do not demonstrate every codec, rich provider metadata, Xtream authentication or catch-up.
6. Open About -> Privacy policy for the offline notice and public policy link.
7. Admin management, optional Settings Lock and parental controls are independent. Do not add a review lock without corresponding reviewer instructions. Fresh Settings Lock is OFF; there is no universal fresh Admin PIN.

For source-restricted features that cannot be demonstrated by this credential-free source, supply a separate authorized reusable reviewer account only in Play Console App access. Do not reuse expired trial credentials or place secrets in these files. Google Play manages updates; there is no direct APK installer.

App access guidance: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455?hl=en
