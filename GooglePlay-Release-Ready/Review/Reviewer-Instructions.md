# Reviewer access instructions

YourIPTV / com.youriptv.app. Developer/support/privacy: YourIPTV, admin@youriptv.us, https://youriptv.us. One application serves phones, tablets, Android TV and Google TV. No YourIPTV registration, purchase or bundled channel subscription is required.

## Prepared legal source and hosting requirement

Review/Demo-Content contains the locally generated legal reviewer package prepared for this completion: synthetic clips, HTTPS M3U/XMLTV, live/movie/episode entries and a guide-refresh helper. No television/movie media, provider subscription or customer credentials are included. Its proposed hosting base is https://youriptv.us/review-media/ and is **PLANNED ONLY**, not a verified reachable deployment.

Before review, the owner must host that package at an approved stable HTTPS location, refresh the XMLTV guide for the review dates, and independently verify playlist, guide, clip and range access outside the home LAN. Do not enter a planned/missing URL in App access as though it works. No website deployment is authorized by this local task. The historical loopback HTTP demo is not usable by remote reviewers or the HTTPS-only production app.

For provider-specific Xtream login, full catalogue, catch-up or other source-restricted features that the synthetic package cannot demonstrate, supply a separate non-personal, authorized, reusable reviewer account. Put real secret credentials only in Play Console App access. Do not reuse the expired private trial account or store credentials in this release directory.

## Steps for App access after hosting is verified

1. Open YourIPTV. Open Settings, then playlist management. Fresh Play Settings opens without a Settings PIN.
2. Add a generic M3U/M3U Plus playlist, name it YourIPTV Review and enter the verified HTTPS playlist address from the hosted Demo-Content package. Add its matching XMLTV guide address if not already advertised by the playlist. Save and refresh. For a separately supplied Xtream test account, use its HTTPS server and credentials.
3. Open the guide/live category, choose a generated demo item, observe the preview and switch to fullscreen. Use touch on a phone or D-pad/OK/Back on TV. After two live items are opened in the same fullscreen queue, Right recalls the prior item and another Right toggles back.
4. Use Update Playlist and Update EPG from the relevant settings; return to the guide. Actual source availability and supplied guide dates apply.
5. Open the generated movie/episode entries. Their bounded synthetic clips test local player paths, not every provider/codec or authentic Xtream/catch-up behavior. Use the authorized reviewer account where real source features require it.
6. Open About -> Privacy policy. The notice is available offline and lists admin@youriptv.us and https://youriptv.us/privacy. The owner must publish the prepared Play-inclusive public policy before submission.
7. Admin management and parental restrictions are separate protections. Do not enable a review lock without also supplying the corresponding authorized instructions. No universal fresh-install Admin PIN is included in the listing.

Google Play manages updates; the app contains no direct APK installer. Offline operation covers saved configuration/interface, not unavailable internet streams. Report errors with the app version and a credential-free description to admin@youriptv.us.

No deployed public demo/reviewer-account pass is claimed in this file. [Google review-access guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455?hl=en).
