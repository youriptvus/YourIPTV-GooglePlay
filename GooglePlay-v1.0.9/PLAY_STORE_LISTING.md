# Proposed listing

App: **YourIPTV**

Category: **Video Players & Editors**, subject to Console eligibility.

Short description (under80 characters):

> Play your authorized IPTV playlists on phones, tablets and Android TV

Full description draft:

> YourIPTV is an independent media player for your own authorized IPTV sources. Bring an Xtream Codes connection, an M3U or M3U Plus playlist, and an XMLTV program guide from a service you have permission to use. Use secure HTTPS source addresses; HTTP-only sources are not supported.
>
> Use one app on your Android phone, tablet, Android TV or Google TV. Touch controls serve mobile devices, while television navigation supports a remote and D-pad.
>
> Browse live categories, favorites and the integrated program guide. Preview channels, switch to fullscreen playback, and return to the previous live channel with Right on a TV remote. Access movies, series and catch-up when your provider supports them.
>
> Manage playlists and guides, choose playback and appearance preferences, and refresh playlist/guide data. Settings are accessible by default on a fresh installation. Optional Settings Lock, Admin security and independent parental controls remain available. Provider connection limits still apply.
>
> Normal player functionality is free. No premium playback gates, trials, playback timers or required YourIPTV subscriptions.
>
> YourIPTV includes no television channels, movies, provider accounts or television subscription. Supply your own legally authorized sources. Content availability, catch-up, guide information and connection limits depend on your provider. Internet access is needed for online sources.

Do not promise recording/reminders while app stopped, every codec/provider, uninterrupted recovery, free paid channels or approval by content owners. No customer provider/account details.
