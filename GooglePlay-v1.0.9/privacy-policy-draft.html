About the player

YourIPTV plays sources you are authorized to use. The app supplies no television channels, television subscription, provider account or content catalogue. No YourIPTV cloud account is required.

Information stored on your device

The app stores playlist names and URLs, provider usernames and passwords where needed, guide configuration and cache, favorites, viewing history, playback state and preferences. It also stores local Admin, Settings and parental security state, restrictions and failed-attempt/lockout information. Provider connection metadata supports provider connection limits.

Database and preferences use Android app-private storage. The app does not additionally encrypt its provider database. PINs are stored as salted hashes; PIN gates are not database encryption. The app disables automatic cloud backup and declares complete Android backup/device-transfer exclusions. These rules do not delete data during compatible in-place updates or remove deliberate manual export.

Connections to your selected services

Your device requests playlists, guides, streams and artwork from the sources referenced by your configuration. Requests may include provider credentials, user-agent headers, content identifiers and requested media or guide resources. Recipients also receive ordinary network information, including IP address and request time. Some provider protocols place credentials in request URLs.

Selected providers and hosts control their own information practices. Review their privacy policies. This Google Play application requires HTTPS for network sources and blocks insecure HTTP connections and HTTPS-to-HTTP redirects. It uses platform certificate validation. Existing HTTP-only sources need a working HTTPS address from their provider before they can connect. HTTPS protects the connection in transit; the selected recipient still receives and processes the information needed to deliver its service.

Developer services and sharing

The audited candidate contains no advertising, analytics, developer account service or automatic crash-reporting SDK. The app does not upload your playlists or viewing history to a developer service and does not sell information. Android and Google Play may process their own device and store information under their policies. When you deliberately export a backup or open media in another application, the chosen document provider or recipient handles the transferred information under its own policy.

Exports and recordings

Explicit JSON backups include readable provider credentials and URLs. PINs and local Settings Lock state are excluded. Protect these files and share them only with trusted recipients. Recordings and exported documents stay in the destination you choose. Uninstalling YourIPTV does not remove files exported outside its private storage.

Retention and deletion

Local configuration remains until changed or removed, relevant app controls delete it, Android storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Guide caches follow configured retention. Clear or uninstall removes app-private information; separately exported files must be deleted at their chosen location. Local deletion does not remove information held by a provider or host; contact that service directly.

Your controls and security

You choose authorized sources, credentials, refresh preferences, parental controls and optional Settings Lock. Fresh Play installations start with Settings Lock disabled; deliberate saved security choices and ambiguous existing protection are preserved on compatible upgrades. Admin authentication and parental restrictions are separate.

Keep your device and provider accounts secure. Do not include passwords, private source URLs or account information in support logs or screenshots. Local PIN checks cannot prevent every form of access to rooted or unlocked devices.
